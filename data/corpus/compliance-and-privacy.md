# Compliance and Privacy

This page summarizes Northstar's compliance programs, which plans they apply to, and how to request compliance documents.

## Compliance at a glance

| Program | Starter | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SOC 2 Type II | SOC 2 Type II covers Starter | SOC 2 Type II covers Pro | SOC 2 Type II covers Enterprise |
| GDPR Data Processing Addendum (DPA) | GDPR DPA available on Starter | GDPR DPA available on Pro | GDPR DPA available on Enterprise |
| HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) | HIPAA BAA not available on Starter | HIPAA BAA not available on Pro | HIPAA BAA available on Enterprise only |
| EU or US data residency | Data residency not available on Starter | Data residency not available on Pro | EU or US data residency available on Enterprise only |

## SOC 2 Type II

Northstar holds a SOC 2 Type II report, and it covers the Northstar service on all plans: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. Customers can request a copy of the current SOC 2 Type II report through support.

## GDPR and the Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

A GDPR Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is available on all paid plans: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. The DPA describes how Northstar processes personal data on your behalf as a data processor. The workspace owner can request the DPA through support; Enterprise customers can also ask their account manager.

## HIPAA and the Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

Northstar signs a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) on the Enterprise plan only. Starter and Pro do not include a BAA, so protected health information (PHI) must not be stored in Starter or Pro workspaces. If your organization needs a BAA, you need an Enterprise contract; the BAA is signed as part of that contract.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers can choose whether their workspace data is stored in the EU or in the US. Data residency is available on the Enterprise plan only and is set as part of your Enterprise contract. Starter and Pro workspaces do not offer a choice of data region.

## Data deletion after closing an account

When an account closes, Northstar keeps the workspace data for a limited retention period and then permanently deletes it. See Cancellation and Data Retention for the exact retention period, and Data Export for how to download your data first.

## Privacy requests

If you need to access, correct, or delete personal data held in your workspace, the workspace owner can usually do this directly in the app. For other privacy requests, contact support from the account owner's email address and we will route the request to the right team.

## Audit logs

Audit logs record sign-ins, sharing changes, and admin actions, and are often requested by compliance teams. Audit logs are available on the Enterprise plan only.
