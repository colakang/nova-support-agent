# Changing Your Plan

This page explains who can change a Northstar plan, and when upgrades, downgrades, and billing-cycle changes take effect.

## Who can change the plan

Only the workspace owner can change the plan, and only in the app under Settings → Billing. Support cannot change a plan or tier on your behalf, even after verifying your identity. If you are not the owner, ask your workspace owner to make the change.

## Upgrading

Upgrades apply immediately. As soon as the workspace owner upgrades, the workspace gets the higher plan's storage, seats, and features. The upgrade is prorated: you are charged only for the remaining part of your current billing cycle at the new price, and the next full charge is at the new plan's price.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Until then, you keep the features and limits of your current plan, and you are not charged the lower price until the next billing cycle starts.

## Before you downgrade

A lower plan has lower limits. Before downgrading, check that your workspace fits the new plan:

- **Storage** — if the workspace uses more storage than the lower plan allows, new files stop syncing until you free up space.
- **Seats** — make sure the number of members fits within the lower plan's seat limit.
- **Features** — Enterprise-only features such as SSO and audit logs are not available on Starter or Pro.

See Storage and Seats for each plan's limits.

## Switching from annual to monthly billing
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro annual customers can switch to monthly billing only at renewal. You can't switch from annual to monthly in the middle of an annual term. The workspace owner can schedule the switch under Settings → Billing, and it takes effect when the current annual term ends. Refunds on Pro annual plans follow the Refund Policy.

## Moving to Enterprise

Enterprise is sold as an annual contract, so it can't be selected in Settings → Billing like Starter and Pro. Ask support and we will connect you with our team to prepare a contract.

## Plan changes on Enterprise
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Changes to an Enterprise plan, including seat counts, add-ons, and moving to a different plan, are governed by your signed annual contract. Contact your named account manager to discuss any change.

## Cancelling instead

If you want to stop using Northstar entirely, see Cancellation and Data Retention. Cancellation also takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
