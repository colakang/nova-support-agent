# Plans and Pricing

Northstar offers three plans: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. Every plan includes cloud file storage, file sharing, team folders, and the Northstar desktop sync app. Plan prices are per workspace, and each plan's price covers every seat up to that plan's seat limit.

## Starter plan

Starter costs $19 per month and is billed monthly only; there is no annual option for Starter. It is built for small teams of up to 5 seats and includes 100 GB of storage, a 2 GB maximum file size, and 30 days of version history. Starter customers get email support.

## Pro plan

Pro costs $79 per month when billed monthly, or $790 per year when billed annually. Pro supports up to 50 seats and includes 1 TB of storage, a 15 GB maximum file size, and 180 days of version history. Pro customers get chat and email support and a 99.9% uptime target.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise is sold as an annual contract, with pricing from $1,500 per month depending on your contract. Enterprise adds unlimited storage and seats, a 50 GB maximum file size, unlimited version history, single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, HIPAA BAA and EU/US data residency options, a 99.95% uptime SLA with service credits, 24/7 phone and chat support, and a named account manager.

## Billing cycles

- **Starter:** monthly billing only, $19 per month.
- **Pro:** monthly billing at $79 per month, or annual billing at $790 per year.
- **Enterprise:** an annual contract, from $1,500 per month.

Each plan is charged at the start of its billing period. See Billing and Invoices for how invoices and failed payments work, and Refund Policy for refund windows on each plan.

## API access

Every plan includes access to the Northstar API. The number of requests you can make per minute depends on your plan; see API Rate Limits for the limit on each plan.

## Changing your plan

Only the workspace owner can change the plan, in the app under Settings → Billing. Support cannot change a plan on your behalf. See Changing Your Plan for when upgrades and downgrades take effect.
