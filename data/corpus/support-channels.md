# Support Channels and Response Times

How you reach Northstar support, and how quickly we respond, depends on your plan.

## Support by plan

| Plan | Support channels | First response time | Support hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Starter support is by email. | Starter emails get a first response within 48 hours. | Starter customers can email at any time. |
| Pro | Pro support is by chat and email. | Pro requests get a first response within 24 hours. | The Pro support team works Monday to Friday, 6am–6pm PT. |
| Enterprise | Enterprise support is by phone and chat, 24/7, plus a named account manager. | Enterprise P1 issues get a response within 1 hour. | Enterprise support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. |

## Starter support
<!-- tiers: starter -->

Starter customers reach our support team by email. We send a first response within 48 hours of receiving your email. Include your account email and any error codes so we can help in one reply.

## Pro support
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro customers can reach our support team by chat or email. We send a first response within 24 hours. The Pro support team is available Monday to Friday, 6am–6pm PT (Pacific Time).

## Enterprise support
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise customers have 24/7 phone and chat support and a named account manager. Your account manager handles contract, billing, and renewal questions, and coordinates with our billing team on refunds and service credits.

### Enterprise priority levels
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

A **P1** issue is a critical problem, such as your workspace being unavailable or sync failing for your whole organization. Enterprise P1 issues receive a response within 1 hour, at any time of day. When you call or chat, tell the agent the issue is P1 so it is routed immediately.

## The Northstar support assistant

The Northstar support assistant is available to customers on every plan. It can answer questions about plans and policies, verify your identity with your account email and support PIN, process eligible refunds, and update your billing email, company name, invoice address, or notification preference.

## Asking for a human

You can ask the assistant for a human at any time. The assistant creates a support ticket with a summary of your conversation and the steps already taken, so you don't have to repeat yourself. A support specialist then follows up through your plan's support channel. Some requests always go to a person: refunds above the support approval limit, a second refund within 30 days, Enterprise refunds, billing disputes, and account owner login email changes.

## Before you contact support

- Check status.northstar.example for known incidents.
- Have your account email and 4-digit support PIN (Settings → Security) ready.
- Note any error code shown by the desktop app.
