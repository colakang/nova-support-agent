# Cancellation and Data Retention

This policy explains how to cancel a Northstar subscription, when the cancellation takes effect, and what happens to your files afterwards.

## How to cancel

The workspace owner can cancel a Starter or Pro subscription in the app under Settings → Billing. Support cannot cancel or change a plan on your behalf. Enterprise workspaces are on annual contracts, so cancellation and non-renewal follow the terms of your signed contract; contact your named account manager.

## When cancellation takes effect

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then you keep full access to your workspace, and you will not be charged again after cancelling. For example, if you cancel a Pro monthly plan 10 days into the billing period, your workspace stays active for the remainder of that period and then closes. If you cancel a Pro annual plan, the workspace stays active until the end of the annual term.

## What happens to your data after the account closes

After your account closes, Northstar retains your data for 30 days. When those 30 days are over, all files, folders, version history, and workspace settings are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by anyone, including Northstar support. Deletion is permanent, so plan your export before the account closes.

## Export before you close

Export everything you want to keep before your billing period ends and the account closes. The workspace owner can download a full copy of the workspace; see Data Export for the steps and what the export contains.

## Charges after cancellation

If you were charged after your cancellation date, that charge is refunded in full, regardless of the normal refund window. Charges dated before your cancellation date follow the normal refund window for your plan: 14 days on Starter, 30 days on Pro monthly, and a prorated refund within 60 days of the charge on Pro annual. See Refund Policy for details.

## Refunds when you cancel

Cancelling does not automatically refund the current billing period. If the latest charge is still inside your plan's refund window, you can ask support for a refund of that charge as well; the normal refund rules and approval limits apply.

## Cancelling versus downgrading

If cost is the main concern, you may not need to cancel. The workspace owner can move to a lower plan instead under Settings → Billing, and your files stay in your workspace. See Changing Your Plan for when a downgrade takes effect and what to check first.
