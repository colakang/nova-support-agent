# Billing and Invoices

This guide covers how Northstar charges your account, where to find invoices, how to update billing details, and what happens when a payment fails.

## How billing works

Northstar charges the payment method on file at the start of each billing period: every month for Starter and Pro monthly, every year for Pro annual, and according to your signed contract for Enterprise. Each charge creates an invoice with its own invoice number (for example, INV-1234), and a copy of every invoice is sent to your billing email.

## Viewing and downloading invoices

The workspace owner can view and download every invoice as a PDF under Settings → Billing → Invoices. Each invoice shows the invoice number, date, amount, plan, company name, and invoice address. If you need an invoice resent, ask support after verifying your identity.

## Updating billing details

Support can update the following billing details for you after verifying your identity with your account email and 4-digit support PIN:

- **Billing email** — the address that receives invoices and receipts.
- **Company name** — the name printed on your invoices.
- **Invoice address** — the postal address printed on your invoices.
- **Notification preference** — how we send billing notifications: email, SMS, or none.

New invoices use the updated details. Support cannot change your plan or the account owner's login email: plan changes are made by the workspace owner under Settings → Billing, and owner login email changes are handled by our security team.

## Failed payments and the grace period

If a payment fails, your account enters a 7-day grace period. During those 7 days, update your payment method under Settings → Billing so the outstanding invoice can be paid. If the balance is still unpaid when the 7-day grace period ends, the account becomes past due.

## Past-due accounts

A past-due account has an outstanding balance from one or more unpaid invoices whose grace period has ended. While an account is past due, no refunds can be issued until the outstanding balance is paid. The workspace owner can pay the balance under Settings → Billing. If you believe an unpaid invoice is wrong, support passes your dispute to our billing team.

## Duplicate charges

If you see two identical charges for the same billing period, contact support with both invoice numbers. A duplicate charge is refunded in full on any plan, regardless of the normal refund window. See Refund Policy for approval limits.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility, approval limits, and timing are described in the Refund Policy. Refunds are always returned to the original payment method.

## Enterprise invoicing
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise billing follows your signed annual contract. Your named account manager is your first contact for invoice questions, purchase orders, and billing adjustments, and works with our billing team on any Enterprise refund or credit. Support can still update your billing email, company name, invoice address, and notification preference after verification.
