# Refund Policy

When Northstar refunds a charge, and how much. Refund windows are counted from the date of each charge (invoice), not from your sign-up date.

## Refunds at a glance

| Plan and billing cycle | Refund rule |
| --- | --- |
| Starter ($19/month) | Starter charges: full refund within 14 days of the charge date. |
| Pro monthly ($79/month) | Pro monthly charges: full refund within 30 days of the charge date. |
| Pro annual ($790/year) | Pro annual charges: prorated refund of unused full months, only within 60 days of the annual charge. |
| Enterprise (annual contract) | Enterprise refunds: governed by your signed contract, handled by your account manager and billing team, never instantly by support. |
| Duplicate charge (any plan) | Duplicate charges: full refund on any plan, regardless of the refund window. |

## Starter refunds
<!-- tiers: starter -->

Starter is billed monthly at $19. A Starter charge can be refunded in full within 14 days of the charge date. After 14 days, a Starter charge is only refundable if it is a duplicate or was charged after you cancelled. Support can refund up to $19 on Starter immediately, so eligible Starter refunds are completed in the conversation.

## Pro monthly refunds
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro monthly charges ($79) can be refunded in full within 30 days of the charge date. After 30 days, a Pro monthly charge is only refundable if it is a duplicate or was charged after you cancelled. Because $79 is below the $250 support limit, eligible Pro monthly refunds are normally processed immediately.

## Pro annual refunds
<!-- tiers: pro -->

Pro annual plans ($790/year) can receive a prorated refund of the unused full months, but only if you ask within 60 days of the annual charge. Each month is worth one twelfth of the annual charge, and the current month counts as used. Example: 20 days after a $790 annual charge, 11 full months are unused, so the refund is 11 × $65.83 = $724.17. Prorated annual refunds exceed the $250 support limit, so our billing team reviews them within 2 business days. After 60 days, a Pro annual charge is only refundable if it is a duplicate or was charged after you cancelled.

## Enterprise refunds
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise refunds and billing adjustments are governed by your signed contract. Support never issues Enterprise refunds directly, including for duplicate charges. Your named account manager and our billing team handle every Enterprise refund request; support opens the request for you. SLA service credits are separate and are not refunds.

## Approval limits

Support can process refunds of up to $250 immediately (up to $19 on Starter). Larger refunds are reviewed by our billing team within 2 business days.

## Duplicate charges

If you were charged twice for the same thing, the duplicate charge is refunded in full on any plan, regardless of the refund window. Support processes duplicate-charge refunds of up to $500 immediately; larger duplicates are reviewed by our billing team within 2 business days. Enterprise duplicates are handled by your account manager and billing team.

## One refund per 30 days

Support processes at most one refund per account in any rolling 30-day period. If your account already received a refund in the last 30 days, further requests go to our billing team.

## Past-due accounts

If your account is past due, no refunds can be issued until the outstanding balance is paid. If you dispute a charge on your balance, support passes the dispute to our billing team.

## Cancelled accounts

After you cancel, any charge dated after your cancellation date is refunded in full, regardless of the refund window. Charges dated before the cancellation date follow your plan's normal refund window.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method and arrive within 5–10 business days. A refund cannot be sent to a different card or account.

## Requesting a refund

Support first verifies your identity with your account email and 4-digit support PIN (Settings → Security). Include the invoice number if you have it, for example INV-1234.
