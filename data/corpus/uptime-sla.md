# Uptime and Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Northstar's uptime commitment depends on your plan. Only Enterprise includes a contractual SLA with service credits. Live service status for every plan is published at status.northstar.example.

## Uptime commitments by plan

| Plan | Uptime commitment | Service credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Starter has no SLA. | Starter is not eligible for service credits. |
| Pro | Pro has a 99.9% uptime target. | Pro is not eligible for service credits. |
| Enterprise | Enterprise has a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. | Enterprise earns service credits of 10% or 25% of the monthly fee when the SLA is missed. |

## Starter uptime
<!-- tiers: starter -->

The Starter plan does not include an SLA or an uptime commitment, and Starter workspaces are not eligible for service credits. We still work to keep Northstar available for every customer, and incidents affecting Starter workspaces are posted on status.northstar.example.

## Pro uptime
<!-- tiers: pro -->

The Pro plan has a 99.9% uptime target. This is a target, not a contractual SLA, and Pro workspaces do not receive service credits if the target is missed. Incidents affecting Pro workspaces are posted on status.northstar.example.

## Enterprise SLA
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Enterprise workspaces are covered by a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. Uptime is measured over each calendar month. If the SLA is missed, you are eligible for a service credit as described below.

### Enterprise service credit amounts
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

- If monthly uptime is below 99.95%, you receive a service credit of 10% of that month's fee.
- If monthly uptime is below 99.0%, you receive a service credit of 25% of that month's fee.

The 25% credit replaces the 10% credit for that month; the two are not added together.

### Requesting a service credit
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Service credits are not applied automatically. You must request a service credit within 30 days of the downtime, through your named account manager or Enterprise support. Requests made after 30 days are not eligible.

### How service credits are paid
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

Approved service credits are applied to your next invoice. Service credits are not refunds: they are never paid out as cash or returned to your card, and they don't count toward the refund rules in the Refund Policy.

## Checking service status

status.northstar.example shows current incidents, past incidents, and scheduled maintenance for every plan. If the desktop app shows sync error E-5001, check the status page first: it means a server-side sync outage, not a problem with your computer.
