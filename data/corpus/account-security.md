# Account Security and Identity Verification

Northstar support verifies who you are before discussing or changing anything on your account. This page explains how verification works and what support can and cannot change for you.

## Verifying your identity with support

To verify your identity, give support two things: the email address on your Northstar account and your 4-digit support PIN. Until you are verified, support can answer general questions about plans and policies, but cannot share invoices, account details, or make any change to your account.

## Finding your support PIN

Your 4-digit support PIN is shown in the Northstar app under Settings → Security. Northstar support will never tell you your PIN and will never ask for your password. Only share your PIN inside a Northstar support conversation.

## Failed verification attempts

If the email and PIN don't match, you can try again. After 3 failed verification attempts in a single conversation, verification is locked for the rest of that conversation, even if the next attempt is correct. While locked, support can still answer general questions but cannot access or change your account. Check your PIN under Settings → Security and start a new conversation.

## Changes support can make after verification

Once you are verified, support can update these account details for you:

- **Billing email** — where invoices and receipts are sent.
- **Company name** — the name shown on invoices.
- **Invoice address** — the postal address shown on invoices.
- **Notification preference** — email, SMS, or none.

## Changes support cannot make

Support cannot change your plan or tier, even after verification. Only the workspace owner can change plans, in the app under Settings → Billing. Support also cannot change the account owner's login email (see below).

## Changing the account owner's login email

The account owner's login email controls the whole workspace, so changing it requires our security team. After verifying you, support opens a request with the security team, who contact you to complete the change. Support cannot change the owner's login email directly. This is different from the billing email, which support can update after verification.

## Single sign-on and audit logs

Single sign-on (SSO) with SAML or OIDC is available on the Enterprise plan only, and so are audit logs. Starter and Pro workspaces sign in with email and password. Enterprise customers can follow the SSO setup guide to connect their identity provider.

## Keeping your account safe

- Never share your support PIN outside a Northstar support conversation.
- Northstar will never ask for your password.
- If you think someone else knows your support PIN, tell support right away.
